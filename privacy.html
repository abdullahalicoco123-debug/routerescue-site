Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

RouteRescue (“RouteRescue”, “we”, “us”) is an on-demand roadside-assistance app that connects riders and drivers who need help with nearby verified mechanics. This policy explains what information we collect, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to both customers and mechanics using the RouteRescue app.

In plain terms: we ask for your details only to run the service — to verify who people are, to send a mechanic to your location, to agree a price, and to keep both sides safe. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

1. Who we are

The RouteRescue service is operated by the RouteRescue Team, based in Islamabad, Pakistan. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact us at routerescue.pk@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +92 334 2526151.

2. Information we collect

From everyone (customers and mechanics)

  • Phone number — used as your login. We send a one-time code (OTP) to verify it.
  • Name, and optionally an email address and preferred language (English or Urdu).
  • Location — your device’s GPS location, to show nearby help, set the pickup point, and let you track the mechanic. See “Location” below.
  • Device information for notifications — a push-notification token and device platform (Android/iOS), so we can alert you about your request or job.
  • An optional emergency (SOS) contact number, if you choose to add one for the safety features.
  • Ratings, reviews and in-app messages you send about or during a job (these may include text and, where enabled, photos).

From customers

  • Your vehicle details — type (bike, car, rickshaw, etc.), make/model, and number plate, if you add them.
  • Service requests — the problem you report, the request location and address, any note, and an optional photo of the problem.

From mechanics (service providers) — for verification

  • CNIC number and CNIC photograph(s) (and, where required, a selfie with CNIC, shop photo, vehicle photo, or towing licence). This is to confirm a mechanic is a real, identifiable person before they can accept jobs — a core safety measure.
  • Workshop / base location and the service area and services offered.
  • Live location while online — for mobile mechanics who choose to receive jobs on the move, we collect location updates while they are set to “online”, including in the background (see “Location”).
  • Job and earnings records — jobs completed, quotes, payments, and commission owed.

Automatically

  • Basic technical and usage data needed to operate the app (for example, app events, approximate timing, and error information).

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • To match a customer’s request with nearby, suitable, verified mechanics and enable the job (location, chat, calling, price agreement, live tracking).
  • To verify mechanics’ identity and eligibility, and to keep the marketplace safe and trustworthy.
  • To calculate and record pricing, payments, commission and payouts.
  • To provide safety features (trip sharing, SOS) and to investigate complaints, disputes, fraud, or misuse.
  • To send service notifications about your request or job.
  • To improve the reliability and quality of the service.

4. Location information

RouteRescue needs location to work — to place your request, find nearby mechanics, and show live arrival.

  • Customers: we use your location when you open the app and create or track a request.
  • Mobile mechanics: when you switch yourself “online” to receive jobs, the app may collect your location periodically, including in the background (“Allow all the time”), so that nearby requests can reach you even when the app is not open. You can stop this at any time by going “offline” in the app, or by changing the location permission in your phone settings. Shop-based mechanics can instead rely on a fixed workshop location and do not need background tracking.

Live positions are kept only as long as needed to run the job and a short audit window; detailed location history is automatically deleted after 90 days.

5. How we share information

We share only what the service requires:

  • Between the two sides of a job. When a job is accepted, the customer and the matched mechanic see what they need to complete it — for example first name, rating, approximate or live location for tracking, and a way to call or chat. Phone numbers are not shown until a job is accepted.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf, under agreements that limit their use of it, including:
    • Google Firebase — phone-number verification (OTP) and push notifications.
    • Cloudinary — secure storage of images (such as profile and verification photos).
    • Map providers — maps and location features (see map attribution in the footer).
    • Our cloud hosting provider, which stores the app’s database and files.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public, or RouteRescue — for example in response to a lawful request, or to investigate fraud, abuse, or a serious safety incident.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

6. How we protect your information

  • Your CNIC number is encrypted where it is stored.
  • Verification images are kept in private storage and are not publicly accessible.
  • Sign-in uses a one-time code; we never store your raw login code.
  • Access to personal data is limited to what is needed to run and support the service.

No system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. Detailed location history is deleted after 90 days. Some records — such as financial, payment, and dispute records — may be kept for longer where we are required to by law or need them to resolve disputes and prevent fraud. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile in the app, or contact us.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and associated personal data — see our Account Deletion page.
  • Location and notifications: you can change or withdraw these permissions at any time in your phone’s settings (some features will stop working without them).

To make any of these requests, contact us at routerescue.pk@gmail.com.

9. Children

RouteRescue is intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new “Last updated” date, and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
