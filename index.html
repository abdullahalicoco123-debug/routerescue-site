How it works

Help in three simple steps

No searching, no calling around, no pushing your bike to a workshop.

STEP 1

Report the problem

Open the app, drop your location, and tell us what’s wrong — a puncture, a dead battery, a breakdown.

STEP 2

Choose your mechanic

Nearby verified mechanics send offers. You see the price, rating and distance, and pick the one you want.

STEP 3

Help arrives

Track your mechanic live as they come to you. Pay the agreed price, then rate each other.
