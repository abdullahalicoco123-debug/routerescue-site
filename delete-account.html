Delete your account & data

Last updated: 18 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your RouteRescue account and your personal data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what happens.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open RouteRescue and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account will be scheduled for deletion.

Option 2 — By request

If you can’t access the app, email us from the phone number or email on your account, or message us on WhatsApp, and we will verify and process your request:

Email to delete my account

Email routerescue.pk@gmail.com  ·  WhatsApp +92 334 2526151

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we delete or anonymise the personal data linked to your account, including:

  • Your name, phone number, email, and language preference.
  • Your profile and, for mechanics, verification details including CNIC number and uploaded identity images.
  • Your vehicles, saved locations, device/notification tokens, and your emergency (SOS) contact.
  • Your location history.

What we may keep (and why)

Some information may be retained for a limited period where the law requires it or where we need it to complete a transaction, resolve a dispute, or prevent fraud. Where possible, this data is anonymised or disassociated from your identity.

KeptWhyRoughly how long
Financial / payment & commission recordsLegal, tax and accounting obligationsAs required by law
Records needed for an open dispute, complaint, or safety/fraud investigationTo resolve the matter fairlyUntil resolved
Anonymised, aggregated statisticsCannot identify youMay be kept

Timeline

We process verified deletion requests promptly, typically within 30 days. Backups that may still contain your data are cycled out on a rolling basis shortly after.

Request a copy of your data

You can also ask for a copy of the personal data associated with your account. Contact us at routerescue.pk@gmail.com and we will verify your identity and respond.
